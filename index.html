Disease Information

Foot and Mouth Disease

Description: A viral infection that causes sores in the mouth and feet of livestock.

Cure: There is no specific cure; management includes vaccination and supportive care.

Severity Level: Moderate

Brucellosis

Description: A bacterial infection that can affect cattle, sheep, and goats, leading to abortion in pregnant animals.

Cure: Antibiotics can help treat the disease, but prevention through vaccination is key.

Severity Level: Severe

Bovine Tuberculosis

Description: A chronic bacterial infection primarily affecting cattle, leading to weight loss and respiratory issues.

Cure: Long-term antibiotic treatment and culling of affected animals are necessary.

Severity Level: Severe

Johne's Disease

Description: A chronic intestinal infection in cattle caused by Mycobacterium avium subspecies paratuberculosis.

Cure: There is no cure; management focuses on reducing the spread and controlling symptoms.

Severity Level: Moderate

Avian Influenza

Description: A viral infection in birds that can spread to other species, including humans, causing respiratory distress.

Cure: Vaccination and biosecurity measures help control outbreaks.

Severity Level: Severe

Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome (PRRS)

Description: A viral disease affecting pigs, leading to reproductive failure and respiratory issues.

Cure: Supportive care and vaccination help manage the disease.

Severity Level: Severe

Chronic Wasting Disease (CWD)

Description: A neurodegenerative disease affecting deer and elk, causing severe weight loss and abnormal behavior.

Cure: There is no cure; management includes population control and monitoring.

Severity Level: Severe

Scrapie

Description: A fatal neurodegenerative disease in sheep and goats caused by prions.

Cure: There is no cure; affected animals must be culled.

Severity Level: Severe

Sheep Pox

Description: A viral disease causing skin lesions and respiratory problems in sheep.

Cure: Vaccination is the best prevention; supportive care is necessary for affected animals.

Severity Level: Moderate

Newcastle Disease

Description: A contagious viral disease affecting birds, causing respiratory distress and nervous system issues.

Cure: Vaccination and biosecurity measures help prevent outbreaks.

Severity Level: Severe

Contagious Bovine Pleuropneumonia (CBPP)

Description: A severe bacterial respiratory disease in cattle.

Cure: Antibiotics can be effective, but prevention through vaccination is crucial.

Severity Level: Severe

Cure: Vaccination and dietary management can prevent this disease.

Severity Level: Severe

Cure: Supportive care and vaccination are used for prevention.
